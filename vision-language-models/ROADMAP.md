# VLM Track Roadmap

This track is designed to be completed during Month 2 of the program, alongside core Modules 03–05.

| Week | Module | Focus |
|------|--------|-------|
| 5 | [01 — Vision Representation](01-vision-representation/) | Patches, ViT, visual features, resolution strategies |
| 5 | [02 — Contrastive Learning](02-contrastive-learning/) | CLIP, SigLIP, shared embedding spaces |
| 6 | [03 — VLM Architectures](03-vlm-architectures/) | Encoder-projector-LM pipeline, cross-attention |
| 6 | [04 — Multimodal Training](04-multimodal-training/) | Alignment, instruction tuning, data mixtures |
| 7 | [05 — Grounding, Documents, OCR](05-grounding-documents-ocr/) | Document understanding, OCR, grounding |
| 7 | [06 — VLM Evaluation](06-vlm-evaluation/) | Hallucination benchmarks (POPE), grounding |
| 8 | [07 — VLM Serving](07-vlm-serving/) | Visual tokens, memory, TTFT, cost |

## Pacing

- ~2 modules per week, alongside core track work
- Emphasis on understanding architectures and serving implications
- Not a deep training track — focus on engineering literacy
