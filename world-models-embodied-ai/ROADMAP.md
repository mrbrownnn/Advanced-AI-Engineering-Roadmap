# World Models & Embodied AI Track Roadmap

This track is designed to be completed during Month 5 of the program, alongside core Modules 14–16.

| Week | Module | Focus |
|------|--------|-------|
| 17 | [01 — Model-Based Learning](01-model-based-learning/) | Model-based RL intuition, prediction for planning |
| 18 | [02 — Latent World Models](02-latent-world-models/) | Latent dynamics, Dreamer-style systems |
| 19 | [03 — Planning](03-planning/) | Planning with learned models, MPC, policy search |
| 20 | [04 — Vision-Language-Action](04-vision-language-action/) | VLA models, RT-2, embodied foundation models |
| 20 | [05 — Embodied Agents](05-embodied-agents/) | Robot manipulation, navigation, physical interaction |
| 21 | [06 — Simulation & Sim-to-Real](06-simulation-sim2real/) | Physics engines, domain randomization, transfer gap |

## Pacing

- ~1–2 modules per week, alongside core track work
- Focus on architectural literacy: perception → world state → predict → plan → act
- Emphasize differences between digital agent loops and physical embodiment
