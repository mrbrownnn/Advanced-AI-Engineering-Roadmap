# Checkpoint Template

Use this template for module checkpoints. Checkpoints test engineering judgment, not recall.

---

## [Checkpoint Title]

### Scenario

_Describe an ambiguous, realistic engineering situation. Provide enough context for the learner to reason about trade-offs but not so much that the answer is obvious._

### Available Information

- _List what the learner knows (metrics, architecture, constraints)_

### Missing Information

- _What is intentionally withheld or unknown_
- _The learner must identify what additional data they need_

### Constraints

- _SLO targets_
- _Hardware/budget limits_
- _Time constraints_
- _Growth projections_

### Expected Evidence

The learner must produce:

1. Hypotheses (ranked by likelihood)
2. What measurements would discriminate between hypotheses
3. Experimental design
4. Quantitative predictions (before measuring)
5. Actual results with confidence information
6. Engineering decision with trade-off analysis
7. Rollback criteria

### Pass Criteria

_What demonstrates competent engineering judgment at the target level._

### Stretch Criteria

_What would demonstrate proficient or expert judgment._
