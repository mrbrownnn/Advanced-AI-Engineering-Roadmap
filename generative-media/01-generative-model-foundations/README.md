# 01 — Generative Model Foundations

## Purpose

Understand foundational generative modeling concepts: latent variables, probabilistic autoencoding, and the historical evolution from VAEs and GANs to modern score-based and diffusion architectures.

## Topics

- Generative vs discriminative modeling
- Variational Autoencoders (VAEs): encoder-decoder structure, latent distributions, ELBO objective, reparameterization trick
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs): generator vs discriminator min-max game (historical context)
- Why the field transitioned from GANs to diffusion: mode collapse, training instability, sample diversity
- Latent space geometry: smoothness, continuity, and vector arithmetic

## Key Questions

- What is the fundamental difference between explicit density models and implicit density models?
- Why did GANs struggle with mode coverage compared to likelihood-based or diffusion models?
- What core architectural concepts from VAEs remain foundational to latent diffusion models?
